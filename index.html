SOFTWARE + ELECTRONICS

AutoBOM

From a component idea to a circuit you can build. I made an AI assistant inside KiCad that finds parts, generates application circuits, and completes a bill of materials.

  • Native C++ interface: extended KiCad with wxWidgets assistant and BOM-review panels.
  • Node.js backend: connected the editor to a JavaScript service over HTTP/JSON for searches, CAD imports, and circuit generation.
  • Sourcing to schematic: integrated DigiKey and JLCPCB/LCSC data, EasyEDA/SnapMagic imports, and reviewed component placement.

ROBOTICS In development

PickleBot

A robot you can play pickleball against. I'm developing an autonomous opponent that tracks the ball, positions itself, and swings a paddle to return shots.

  • Computer vision: developing ball and court tracking with Python, OpenCV, YOLO, and Roboflow, using a Raspberry Pi 5 with a Hailo-8L AI accelerator.
  • Embedded control: Raspberry Pi–ESP32 communication over UART, PWM motor/servo control, and Bluetooth for manual driving.
  • Power electronics & mechatronics: custom power/control PCB, mechanical assemblies, and custom-designed brackets integrating motors, actuators, aluminum extrusion, and 3D-printed parts.
